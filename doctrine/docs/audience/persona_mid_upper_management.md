**Category:** `INTERNAL Stakeholder`  
**Audience Type:** Delivery leadership overseeing migration programs and platform investments  
**Primary Goal:** Ensure validation tooling reduces risk, cost, and timeline for OSX to ABACUS migrations while maintaining governance.  
**Reading Context:** Executive and architecture board materials, roadmap briefs, and investment approvals.

## Overview

* **Role Focus:** Portfolio and delivery sponsor for regulatory migration programs.  
* **Primary Function:** Approve funding, set priorities, and evaluate delivery risk against customer commitments.  
* **Environment / Context:** Balances commercial targets, regulatory deadlines, and cross-team capacity constraints.

## Core Motivations

* **Professional Drivers:** Protect delivery predictability and customer confidence while scaling migration throughput.  
* **Emotional or Cognitive Drivers:** Pragmatic, risk-aware; prefers clear trade-offs and measurable outcomes.  
* **Systemic Positioning:** Acts as escalation point for delivery blockers and accountable owner for program KPIs.
