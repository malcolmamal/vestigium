## Overview

* **Emotional or Cognitive Drivers:** Risk-averse but pragmatic; values transparency and vendor accountability.  
* **Systemic Positioning:** Acts as executive sponsor and gatekeeper; can escalate issues or greenlight adoption.

## Narrative Summary

Carla shepherds the organization through migrations that regulators scrutinize closely. She delegates hands-on comparisons to analysts but must publicly vouch for the results. She wants confidence that Regnology owns the verification process, provides immutable evidence, and respects her team’s limited time. When she trusts the validator, she becomes an advocate for expanding its use to additional regulatory reports.
